# Gap register - the clinical tick list for v2.0.0

One row for every gap in the register of the approved v2 plan, the same codes as [WHO_ALIGNMENT_2026.md](WHO_ALIGNMENT_2026.md): F1-F14 (fidelity to the WHO Labour Care Guide form), N1-N5 (the 2025 WHO guidance), S1-S13 (safety-grade defects of v1). For each: the gap in one line (v1 behaviour in parentheses), the WHO source, the status with the milestone that closed it, where it lives in the code, and the automated test that proves it. It is the tick list for the release pull request and the starting point for the clinical panel.

State at the start of milestone M6, 1 October 2026 (branch `m6-verify-publish`, `npm test` 445 of 445). Every test name below is quoted exactly and was checked against the test files and the test run. "Browser walk" marks screen behaviour that no unit test reaches.

**Summary: 32 items - 30 done, 2 partly (F10, S10), 0 open.** "Done" means built and tested, not clinically validated: every rule still goes to the Ethiopian obstetric and midwifery panel (section 4), and the app is not for facility use until the panel has signed off. Related items still open are in section 5.

**Sources.** Form = WHO Labour Care Guide form (2020). Manual = WHO labour care guide: user's manual (2020), tables by number. IRP = WHO labour care guide: implementation resource package (2025). PPH 2025 = WHO/FIGO/ICM consolidated guidelines on postpartum haemorrhage (2025). rec N = WHO 2018 intrapartum recommendation N, reproduced in the manual's annex. Local text copies, not committed: `_sources/lcg-form.txt`, `lcg-manual.txt`, `lcg-irp.txt`; the PPH 2025 text is not among them (section 4.3).

### 4.2 Choices recorded for the panel (not marked in the code)

- Rules kept from v1 beyond the WHO Alert column: severe FHR, pulse, BP and temperature tiers; moulding ++; ungraded meconium on v1 records; the second-stage early warning; rupture of membranes over 18 h; oxytocin above 60 drops/min; the 8-hour national latent-phase rule (F11); the admission risk referral; APGAR below 7.
- Contraction findings (2 or fewer, or more than 5, per 10 min; shorter than 20 s or longer than 60 s) alert at the first count with a prompt to count again, where Manual Table 6 verifies over another 10 minutes first (F8); more than 5 is a danger alert at once.
- Every new abnormal value asks for acknowledgement again: up to 12 an hour for a persistent abnormal FHR in the second stage.
- Wizard defaults (supportive care Y, posture upright, no decelerations, caput and moulding 0) are committed on Next and stored as `defaulted`; the birth outcome starts on live birth, a stillbirth's timing is pre-filled from fresh or macerated, and the admission form pre-fills the labour onset time as 2 h ago.
- Labour findings, thick meconium included, close at birth.
- Whether alerts closed by a void or by restaging count in the audit's alert handling.
- The PPH signs (pulse above 100, shock index above 1, systolic below 100, diastolic below 60) replaced the plan's placeholders (pulse 120, systolic 90).
- The Amharic drafts; alert titles and advice stay in English until the panel validates a translation.

## 5. Still open

1. **F10, partly.** IV fluids and other medicines are recorded only when given (IV fluids drawn as Y); the form's hourly N is never recorded, and the audit's medication section covers oxytocin only.
2. **Beside S5.** Medication entries and notes could not be voided from the screens. Hardened in M6 on this branch, not yet merged: `js/record.js` voidMedication(), voidNote(); record: `M6: voidMedication keeps the entry struck through; an unknown or voided id, no reason or no initials is refused`, `M6: voidNote keeps the note struck through; refuses an unknown or voided id, no reason, no initials, and the notes the app writes`; void-outputs: `M6: the chart leaves out a voided medicine, a voided oxytocin record and a voided note, on both layouts`. They are voided, not corrected: a wrong one is voided and entered again.
3. **Beside S8.** Labour alerts already open when a referred woman left stayed open; only the time alerts closed. Hardened in M6 on this branch, not yet merged: record: `M6: at her departure her open labour findings close at the departure time; maternal findings stay open`.
4. Print on a browser without CSS `:has()` (fallback classes exist): to check in the M6 browser walk.
5. **S10, partly: one origin for every site of the account.** GitHub Pages serves all project sites of the account from one origin, `https://drtemesgen.github.io`, so v2 shares its Cache Storage with Parthograph v1 and the BBI platform, and each of the three service workers deletes every cache but its own when it activates. Opening v2 once on a tablet that runs v1 deletes v1's offline copy; installing v1 or a BBI update deletes v2's, which then fails offline, or is refilled file by file through the browser cache instead of as one release. Proposed in M6 for `sw.js`: delete only `lcg-` caches and refill the whole shell on a miss. The other two service workers, or a host name of its own for v2, need the owner's go.
6. A load that no service worker controls (a browser without service workers, a private window, a revisit after a failed install) can combine modules of two releases within the 10 minutes after a release, the `max-age=600` GitHub Pages sends; a first visit cannot. A version query on `js/app.js` would not prevent it, because its imports are fetched without the query.
